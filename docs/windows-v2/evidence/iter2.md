**Aesthetic read:** A quiet courtyard aesthetic: chalky cream, forest green, terracotta controls, bookish serifs and sunlit Islamic architecture. The first impression is welcoming and contemplative; the second is a collection of editorial cards without a sufficiently disciplined desktop product system.

**Top gaps** (max 5, most important first)

1. **[Whole app / composition and hierarchy]**: The cream-and-serif direction feels generic because almost everything receives the same rounded-panel treatment. Home presents five equally strong brown actions; Practice has two introductions before the choices; Hijri wastes roughly 120px above its content. **Fix:** establish one content origin, 32px beneath a consistent 64px header, with 32px side gutters. Use flat sections and dividers for routine content, reserving enclosed surfaces for meaningful groups. On Home, make next prayer and the relevant adhkar session dominant; demote calendar, library and tasbih to compact links. Replace Practice’s illustrated hero and manifesto with one heading, one sentence and the choices.

2. **[Library / broken card proportions]**: Eight narrow columns produce clipped category labels, awkward title wrapping and enormous cards around small covers. “31 Benefits…” is visibly cut off inside a word. Browsing becomes deciphering. **Fix:** use five columns at this width, with a minimum card width of 240px and 20px gaps. Set titles to 17–18px/24px, authors to 14px/20px, and cover areas to approximately 180px high. Remove category ribbons—the category sidebar already supplies that structure. Delete the duplicate Library heading.

3. **[Hijri and Prayer / information clarity]**: The calendar scatters isolated date squares across oversized gutters; its event dots have no visible key. Both calendar and prayer navigation show right-pointing arrows on the left and left-pointing arrows on the right despite the English interface. Prayer’s lower timeline also disagrees with the timetable above: Fajr 05:42 versus 05:41, for example. **Fix:** build a contiguous seven-column calendar with 1px rules, 80–88px rows and explicit event labels or a concise legend. Make both numbers on the selected date white. Use left chevrons for previous and right chevrons for next in English. Present one consistent set of prayer times; consolidate the three Monthly entry points and repeated update information.

4. **[Adhkar and Tasbih / mouse and keyboard affordances]**: Adhkar separates names from their actions by over 1,000px, while repeated filled buttons dominate the list and tiny edit/delete icons look incidental. Tasbih’s count and increment button are disconnected by a large gap, with settings floating separately above. **Fix:** constrain Adhkar to approximately 1,120px, use 56–64px rows, and place counts directly beside names. Give Start a quieter outlined treatment and consolidate management actions into one labelled menu. On Tasbih, bring counting within 24px of the dial or make the dial the counting target; keep settings and secondary actions in that same group. Show a concise keyboard hint and a consistent 2px focus outline.

5. **[Typography and Settings / inconsistent reading scale]**: Small metadata and actions repeatedly fall to roughly 10–12px, while the library uses oversized titles. Home’s Arabic remembrance crosses an image fade and ends with an awkward isolated fragment. Settings spends most of its viewport advertising six themes, yet the language selector visibly omits French. **Fix:** standardize body text at 15–16px and secondary text at 13–14px; reserve serif type for page titles and devotional content. Give Arabic a plain reading surface, approximately 28–32px type and generous line spacing. Compress theme selection into short rows with small previews, put mode beside it, and include Français in the language control.
