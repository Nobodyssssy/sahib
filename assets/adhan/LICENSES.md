stored locally with the app and are never fetched from a third-party CDN at runtime.

## Rejected sources

- `Kiwifu/adhan-mp3` on GitHub was reviewed and **not** used. The repository has no
  licence file, so its collection-level claim cannot be verified for redistribution.
- Internet Archive Fajr recordings without an item-level licence were not used.

CC0 places the recordings in the public domain. Attribution is not required by the
licence, but the recordists and source pages are credited here and in Settings → About
as a matter of provenance.
